03 / Make the audit reproducible

A compact, explicit input format

One row per observation. Probabilities must refer to the positive class and come from a frozen model. Keep calibration labels out of fitting, and evaluation labels out of both fitting and calibration.

ColumnMeaningAccepted values
row_idUnique observation identifierRecommended; detects repeated IDs
studyIndependent source or study groupNonempty text
yMeasured binary outcome0 or 1
pPredicted probability of label 1Number from 0 to 1
rolePartition used for this auditcalibration, evaluation; optional training

Different column names can be mapped after upload. Optional training rows support source-overlap checks; they do not train a model. If provided, dose_ug_ml is screened for unusually high values without changing them. Do not upload direct identifiers of research participants.

Interpretation

Separate the targets

Row coverage, whole-source coverage and error among singleton decisions answer different questions. Always report their denominators.

Check the assumptions

Source IDs do not automatically establish independent laboratories. Distribution shifts and selective reporting can invalidate exchangeability.

Export the evidence

The JSON report stores configuration, input fingerprint when available, thresholds, selected calibration rows and diagnostic summaries. CSV exports preserve observation-level sets.
